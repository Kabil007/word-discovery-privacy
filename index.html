Privacy Policy

Word Discovery: Search & Find

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use Word Discovery: Search & Find ("the Game"), developed and published by F11.

By using the Game, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Word Discovery is designed primarily as a locally played word-search puzzle game. Game progress and gameplay information may be stored locally on your device.

We do not require you to create an account or provide your name, address, or other personal information directly to play the Game.

However, third-party services used by the Game may automatically collect certain information, such as device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location derived from network information, app interactions, diagnostics, and other information required to provide their services.

2. Advertising

Word Discovery displays advertisements using Google AdMob. The Game may include banner advertisements, interstitial advertisements, and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect and process information such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location, ad interactions, and diagnostic information in order to provide, measure, secure, and personalize advertising where permitted.

Rewarded advertisements are optional and may allow players to receive in-game benefits such as hints or additional hearts after watching an advertisement.

For more information about how Google handles information, please review Google's Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. In-App Purchases

Word Discovery may offer optional in-app purchases, including game items and an option to remove certain advertisements.

Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. We do not directly receive or store your full payment card or banking information. Payment processing and purchase transactions are handled by Google Play according to Google's applicable terms and privacy practices.

4. Game Progress and Local Storage

The Game may store information locally on your device to provide gameplay functionality. This may include:

  • Current and completed levels
  • Unlocked level progression
  • Hints and reserve hearts
  • Game settings
  • Audio and vibration preferences
  • Purchase or entitlement state used by the Game

Local game information may be removed if you clear the application's data or uninstall the Game, except for purchases that may be restorable through Google Play where supported.

5. Third-Party Services

The Game may use third-party services that process information according to their own privacy policies.

These services may include:

6. Advertising Choices and Consent

Where required by applicable law, users may be provided with privacy choices or consent options relating to advertising and data processing.

Advertising availability and personalization may depend on your region, device settings, consent choices, and Google's advertising policies.
